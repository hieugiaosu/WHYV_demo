Wanna hear your voice? A sample is all we need!

Anonymous Submission to INTERSPEECH 2025

"In the sea of voices, I wanna hear yours."

Abstract: Research on audio clue-based target speaker extraction (TSE) has primarily focused on modeling mixture and reference speech, achieving impressive results in English, largely due to the abundance of available datasets. However, cross-lingual speech properties have received less attention. Low-resource languages, often lacking large-scale annotated datasets and sufficient linguistic resources, pose significant challenges for developing effective TSE systems. To address this gap, we introduce WHYV (Wanna Hear Your Voice), a novel approach designed for cross-lingual TSE model transfer without fine-tuning. At the core of WHYV is a gating mechanism that dynamically modulates specific frequencies based on the target speaker's acoustic features, enabling robust zero-shot cross-lingual adaptation. This innovation reduces reliance on language-specific information, allowing a model trained on one language to generalize effectively to other languages without additional training. Our model achieves remarkable results, demonstrating superior zero-shot cross-lingual performance compared to existing models: 13.8 dB on the Libri2Mix dataset (mix-both mode), 18.1 dB (mix-clean mode), and 14.8 dB on a Vietnamese test set. We provide the live demo at: (https://anonymous.4open.science/w/WHYV/).

Gradio Playground

Instructions for Using the Playground
In the playground, you have two options:
  • Mixing Tool: Use this tool to mix two separate speech files. Select the appropriate SNR rate and press Mix Voices.
  • Upload: Alternatively, you can upload two separate speech files.
For the target speaker extractor, you can either:
  • From Mixing Tool: Use the mixed audio from the tool by selecting From Mixing Tool.
  • Upload Mixed Audio: Choose this option and upload a mixed audio file.
Don't forget to add 4-second reference audio by uploading it!
