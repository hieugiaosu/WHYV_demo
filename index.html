Language-Agnostic Target Speaker Extraction with Zero-Shot Generalization to Low-Resource Languages

"In the sea of voices, I wanna hear yours."

Recent research on audio-based target speaker extraction (TSE) has achieved remarkable performance in high-resource languages such as English or Chinese. However, low-resource languages often lack large-scale high-fidelity datasets and sufficient linguistic resources, which raises significant challenges for developing effective TSE systems. To bridge this gap, we introduce a novel gating mechanism that effectively exploits acoustic information, thereby reducing dependence on linguistic information. In this work, we address the challenge of applying a well-trained model in one language to other languages without requiring additional training while maintaining consistent performance. With this approach, our model achieves remarkable performance on multiple settings with SDR of 13.8 dB and 18.1 on Libri2Mix dataset mix-both mode and mix-clean mode, respectively. Especially, we also examine the performance of our model on a low-resource language (Vietnamese) and achieve SOTA results with SDR of 14.8 dB without any additional training stages. These results demonstrate superior zero-shot adaptation to different languages compared to baselines. We provide the live demo, codebase, and benchmark at our repository

Instructions for Using the Playground
In the playground, you have two options:
  • Mixing Tool: Use this tool to mix two separate speech files. Select the appropriate SNR rate and press Mix Voices.
  • Upload: Alternatively, you can upload two separate speech files.
For the target speaker extractor, you can either:
  • From Mixing Tool: Use the mixed audio from the tool by selecting From Mixing Tool.
  • Upload Mixed Audio: Choose this option and upload a mixed audio file.
Don't forget to add 4-second reference audio by uploading it!
